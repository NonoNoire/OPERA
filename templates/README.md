# Templates

This directory contains shareable OPERA templates. They are designed for the public method and must not be copied automatically from a personal Obsidian vault without checking for local conflicts.

## Capture templates

- `daily-checkout.md`: the six-question daily check-out template, including the research-state and critical-note checkpoints.
- `opera-export-block.md`: the structured block used to move from conversation to Markdown capture.
- `daily-research-log.md`: a richer daily log capturing observations, questions, hypotheses, connections, and decisions.
- `interview-trace.md`: semi-structured interview guide with thematic areas, follow-up prompts, and post-interview notes.

## OPERA Studio templates

- `studio-work.md`: persistent artwork, series, installation, design investigation, or work-seed record.
- `studio-experiment.md`: bounded practical test or iteration, including the distinction between technical events and artistic selection.
- `studio-asset-manifest.md`: individual or batch manifest for photographs, drawings, scans, meshes, textures, video, CAD, fabrication files, and documentation.

Studio templates implement the four-entity model documented in `docs/opera-studio-architecture.md`: entry, work, experiment, and asset. Relations are derived from explicit identifiers rather than inferred from prose.
