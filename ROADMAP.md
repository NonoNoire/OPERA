# OPERA Roadmap

This roadmap describes the provisional development phases of OPERA. Version names are working labels and may change as the system develops.

## v0.1 Genesis

Initial repository and methodological scaffold.

Planned scope:

- define the repository architecture;
- establish the first documentation files;
- clarify the methodological principles of OPERA;
- separate the shareable system from the private research vault;
- record the first Genesis Snapshot.

## v0.2 Daily Workflow

Development of the daily documentation procedure.

Potential scope:

- daily note structure;
- recurring research prompts;
- observation capture protocol;
- distinction between observation, question, hypothesis, experiment, and reflection;
- weekly review routine.

## v0.3 Conversational Capture

Development of the conversational capture workflow.

Potential scope:

- check-in and check-out templates;
- OPERA export block;
- Codex extraction procedure;
- Markdown capture notes;
- GitHub-to-Obsidian synchronisation pattern;
- stable checkpoint identifiers.

## v0.4 Knowledge Graph

Development of the conceptual and ontological structure.

Potential scope:

- controlled vocabulary;
- relation types;
- research objects and entities;
- tagging principles;
- graph navigation strategy;
- criteria for linking notes across disciplines.

## v0.5 Automation

Integration of lightweight automation.

Potential scope:

- reminders for daily capture;
- automated review prompts;
- archival routines;
- metadata checks;
- calendar integration;
- optional motivational research notes.

## v0.6 Stable Beta

Sustained testing across real research activity.

Potential scope:

- refinement after extended use;
- documentation of known limitations;
- example workflows;
- anonymised use cases;
- preparation of public-facing documentation.

## v1.0 Public Release

Possible publication of a stable version.

Potential scope:

- finalised documentation;
- licensing decision;
- public examples;
- installation guide;
- contribution guidelines;
- release notes.

## Future Development Space

Future versions may include:

- research interview integration;
- qualitative coding support;
- exhibition documentation workflows;
- digital twin and material-process tracking;
- export procedures for thesis writing;
- structured bibliographic integration.
